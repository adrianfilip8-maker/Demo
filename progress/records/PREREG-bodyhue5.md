Sealed **before** the candidate texture is regenerated and before any capture. `shots/bodyhue5/`
does not exist; `sly_body_fix.png` on disk is still the −21.1° artefact at the time of writing.

This is the re-seal that `PREREG-bodyhue4.md` §5 anticipated: *"the next step is to re-derive the
render's hue shift per lighting condition … and then re-seal. That is a re-derivation from a sound
measurement, not a nudge toward a bar."* The sound measurement now exists (`RESULT-bodyshift.md`).

## 1. Scope: the two close-ups are the whole battlefield, and that is now a finding, not a compromise

§281: the albedo governs the costume's screen hue **only** at ≤ 4 m — `sly-closeup` and
`sly-perch`. Every mid/far canonical shot is NONLINEAR or below the mask floor, so no texture can
address them, in this engine or any. Earlier seals scoped to these two shots because of container
rebuilds; this one scopes to them because they are **all the shots the deliverable can touch**.
The mid-range blue attractor stays open as §281 / task #23 and this seal claims nothing about it.

## 2. The candidate, derived before it exists

From `RESULT-bodyshift.md`'s registered outputs (arm-mean per-shot offsets, fresh boots, P-S 2/2):

    dS(sly-closeup) = mean(-0.8, -1.0) = -0.9°
    dS(sly-perch)   = mean(-7.5, -8.8) = -8.2°   (midrange of the two: -4.5°)

    target albedo   = 213.5° - (-4.5°) = 218.0°
    rotation        = 218.0° - 229.3°  = -11.3°   (raw circular median 229.3°, run-4 scorer)

> **Candidate:** `sly_body_fix.png` regenerated by `tools/slybody.mjs --deg=-11.3` — same
> operator, same costume window (hue 190–270°, sat > 0.15, opaque), same sat/value invariant
> (|Δ mean| ≤ 0.01 or the tool exits non-zero). Derive-time check: the new texture's costume
> median must land **218.0° ± 0.5°**. `tools/slybody.mjs`'s header and `tests/slybody.test.mjs`'s
> hue pin move to the new derivation in the same commit — the old pin encodes §277's +5.6°, which
> `RESULT-bodyhue.md` refuted.

The −21.1° history, for the record: it pre-compensated for a +5.6° render shift that was an
average over shadow-dominated outliers. The corroborating 207.8° hand-authored original loses its
witness status with the +5.6 — the agreement was real but both numbers descended from the same
frame population. Nothing in run 4's P1 is retracted: rotation preservation at close range is
exactly what this candidate relies on.

## 3. Instrument

`tools/bodyshift.mjs` with `SANDS_OUT=shots/bodyhue5`, shots `sly-closeup,sly-perch`, fresh
frames. Same-boot A/B, mask ≥ 18, circular median, camDist recorded. Scored by
`tools/bodyhue5score.mjs` against this section only.

## 4. Registered gates and predictions

Per shot: **CAL-1** mask ≥ 0.15 % of frame (must fire) · **CAL-2** `sha(A) ≠ sha(B)`, modes echo
(must fire) · **CAL-R** |dS_A − dS_B| ≤ 2.0° (bodyshift measured 0.2° and 1.2° here; failure at
close range would be new physics and voids the shot).

### P1 — mechanism *(run 4's, at the new constant)*
Circular median hue moves **−11.3° ± 4.0°** from A to B, on each shot.
- **F1:** outside refutes rotation preservation at the new hue — the one thing run 4 proved.

### P2 — target *(the band verbatim, fifth seal)*
Arm B's circular median within **213.5° ± 6.0°**, on each shot.
Point predictions, written now: **217.1°** (`sly-closeup`), **209.8°** (`sly-perch`) — margins
of ~2.4° inside the band on both sides.
- **F2:** either shot outside refutes the offset-stability assumption (that dS measured on
  albedos at 229.3°/208.2° holds at 218.0°).

### Outcomes
`PASS` (P1 ∧ P2 on both shots) · `MECHANISM-ONLY` (P1 met, P2 refuted) · `FAIL` (P1 refuted) ·
`VOID` (any must-fire gate null).

**PASS — and only PASS — flips `bodyMode()`'s default from `'raw'` to `'fix'`**, with
`?body=raw` remaining as the escape lever and the lever test updated to pin the new default in
the same commit. The flip's claim is scoped to close-range costume hue; §281 remains open.

## 5. The expected outcome, written down in advance

**PASS**, and for a structural reason absent from every earlier seal: this is the first candidate
whose target was derived from per-shot offsets measured by the same instrument that will score it,
reproduced across fresh boots to 0.4° (P-S). If it fails P2 anyway, the likely lesson is that dS
drifts with albedo hue even at close range — CAL-R's 2.0° cap is the tripwire for that. Forecast
record to date: 1/1 (bodyhue4), 0/1 (bodyshift).
