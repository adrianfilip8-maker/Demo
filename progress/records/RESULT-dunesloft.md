### 3.1 Control A — the predicate fires on empty sand

Identical predicate on flat background sand patches of the same size, containing no subject:

```
sand patch (430,250)   cols 64  maxStep 23.1  CARRIES
sand patch (470,200)   cols 64  maxStep  2.4  clean

```

**Three of four featureless regions "carry a terminator".**

### 3.2 Control B — the scatter null does not kill it

A terminator is by definition a contiguous spatial step, so shuffling a column's values must
destroy it. Shuffled, **10 of 10 flanks still carry.** The predicate is reading the *value
distribution* and not spatial structure at all. This is the more serious of the two controls: A
says the predicate is unspecific, B says it is not measuring the thing its name claims.

### 3.3 The population is 80% dune — look at the mask

`EVID-dunesloft-avenue-mask-2x.png` (landed beside this record; ROI `(0,240) 700×400` of the graded
frame, **at 2× — the read below is stated at that magnification**) paints every scanned pixel
magenta. **Two patches land on a sphinx body. The remaining eight land off it** — on bare dune below
and right of the crest, on the crest line itself, or clipping a plinth edge. Whatever those eight
columns measured, it was not a sphinx flank.

So L2's PASS is: an unspecific predicate, insensitive to spatial structure, run mostly on sand.

### 3.4 Why the dry run passed while measuring the wrong thing

The dry-run positive control reproduced its expected ~1.9 L noise floor exactly, and I read that as
the instrument being calibrated. It is not evidence of anything. `plateaus()` segments a column into
runs whose max−min ≤ `win` (2.5 L), then reports the **median spread inside those runs** as `noise`.
That quantity is measured *within segments the algorithm has already chosen to be flat*, so it is
bounded above by 2.5 L by construction and was guaranteed to come out small.

The adaptive threshold is inert as a consequence: `thresh = max(6.0, 3 × noise)` with `noise ≤ 2.5`
can only ever land in `[6.0, 7.5]`, and the table above shows it pinned at exactly **6.00 in every
scored row**. The "3× measured noise" adaptivity never engaged.

> **A positive control proves an instrument can fire; only an absence control proves it can
> decline.** L2 had the first and not the second, and shipped a PASS on that basis.

## 6. The finding that is not about shading — the avenue is buried in its own hero shot

Ten flanks pass the frustum test. **Two are visible.** The foreground dune crest runs diagonally
across the frame and occludes the entire near and middle avenue; the only sphinxes a viewer can
read are the two at frame-left, in shadow, at the greatest distance of the ten.

This is why `dunes` structurally cannot answer the form question: the surface under test is behind a
dune. It is also a level-design defect independent of any measurement — **the shot that exists to
show the sphinx avenue is ~70% avenue-free**, and critic pass 6 will be looking at the same buried
avenue, so anything it says about the sphinxes will be about those two shadowed animals.

The fix is one of: move the camera, lower or reshape the dune, or move the avenue. That choice
spans `src/core/Shots.js` (not mine), `Terrain.js` (not mine) and `Props.js`/`Statues.js` (mine), so
it needs routing rather than a unilateral edit. **Proposed, not actioned.**

## 7. §71.6 — the budget re-weighting was based on a mis-scaled number. Withdrawn.

The coordinator flagged this before I acted on it. **The flag is correct and I am unwinding the
re-weighting.**

I read this run's own log — `canon draws=261 tris=1566197` — plus the in-flight capture, and
concluded triangles were "the live breach at ~1.6–1.7 M against 1.2 M". That compares two different
quantities:

- **`report.json.triangles` is an all-passes, per-frame submission counter.** Verified in code, not
  inferred: `Engine.js:67` sets `renderer.info.autoReset = false`; `Engine.js:267` calls
  `info.reset()` immediately *before* the PostFX/render call; `Engine.js:273–274` reads
  `info.render.calls` / `info.render.triangles` after it. So one frame's shadow, outline, AO,
  bloom and composite passes all accumulate into the figure. §8 documents a **4.5–5.4× pass
  multiplication**; §51.3 measured **3.1×** on this scene.
- **§1's budget is `≤ 250 draw calls, ≤ 1.2M triangles visible`** — the *visible* column. §51.3
  already separates the two and rules §1 is scored main-view: **93 draws / 0.668 M** worst case,
  against a counted **265 draws / 1.747 M**.

This is exactly **§56.4**, which I established myself for `drawCalls` — same quantity, wrong
comparison — and then repeated on `triangles` one section later.

For 1.566 M counted to breach 1.2 M visible the pass multiplier would have to be under **1.31×**,
against a measured 2.62× (1.747/0.668) and a documented 4.5–5.4×.

### The measurement, rather than the rescaling argument

A rescaling argument is not a measurement, so I took one. `scratchpad/archbudget.mjs` computes the
main-view numerator headlessly via `tools/lvl.mjs` — **no lock, no GPU, ~40 s** — using
`tools/budget.mjs`'s own cull rule (world-space bounding sphere against the camera frustum,
`frustumCulled === false` always billed, collision proxies never billed):

```
architecture range: 9–44 draws (budget 250), 0.124–0.323M tris (budget 1.200M)
built total, unculled: 44 meshes, 0.315M tris
```

**Architecture's worst case over all fifteen cameras is 44 draws and 0.323 M triangles — 18% of the
draw budget and 27% of the triangle budget.** The module I own, the one I was about to start
deleting geometry from to save triangles, is nowhere near the constraint.

**Consequence for the work queue:** the tension between problem 1 (spend triangles on smooth
normal gradients where a terminator should read) and problem 3 (pay for it by merging, instancing
and deletion) is **much weaker than I concluded**, and in architecture it is currently not binding
at all. Triangle reduction drops down the queue; draw-call merging keeps its place on its own
merits. I have not deleted any geometry on the strength of the mis-scaled figure — the flag arrived
before the edit, which is the whole value of it having been raised early.

**What is still owed:** the scene-total main-view figure. `archbudget.mjs` gives the numerator for
architecture only; the denominator includes Props, Statues, Terrain, Vegetation, Water, the
character and the guards, and the last direct scene-total measurement (0.668 M) predates this tree.
`tools/budget.mjs` needs a booted renderer, so **that measurement should ride along on the next
capture already holding the lock rather than spend a lock of its own.**
