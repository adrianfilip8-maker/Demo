     - albShadow / albAmb mix alb toward its own LUMA (uShadowSat -0.35, :173) — a channel-
       MIXING map, not a per-channel scale.
   So D = (linear non-diagonal map of alb) + (a constant independent of alb), and D_R/D_G is
   not a function of alb_R/alb_G alone. The transfer is not an operation this shader supports.
   That defect is independent of the `sh` premise and survives any re-basing of line 1.
