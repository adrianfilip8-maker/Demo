# NOTE — tail palette decision (task #19; CHARACTER's call per NOTE-blueskew-comparison.md)

Two questions were formally routed here by the coordinator out of the blueskew close. Both are
decided below and neither moves a knob in this tree. Recorded so the next K-pass scores
against the authored intent instead of against a prose assumption.

## 1. Cream + navy IS the authored intent. The K4 expectation updates; no PAL edit.

`PAL.cream 0xe4dfcb` is not a tail colour — it is the light end of the character's one value
ladder, shared by the muzzle, the chest V and the tail bands by authored design (its own
comment says so). Moving the tail bands toward grey either breaks that family (tail-only
grey against a cream muzzle) or drags the muzzle grey with it, which is wrong in every
reference frame that exists of this character.

Direction grounds, not just bookkeeping ones: the body fur is authored cool slate (§2.1),
and the cream bands + gold hardware are the character's only warm accents. Under §2.2's
violet-teal shadow hue, grey has no warm floor to hold and renders as the "violet concrete"
failure KNOWN_ISSUES §3 documents on sandstone. Cream survives a golden-hour key; grey
merely reports it.

K4's "grey" was prose written against cap5 frames rendered through the grade defect that
both blind registrations independently convicted (shade-side sign flip = grade-owned, not
albedo). Re-authoring the palette to match a defective grade is exactly the reflexive tuning
the comparison note warns against, and it would also invalidate TEXTURES' registered
neutral-arm bands (cream b−r ∈ [−34, −6]), which are the formal settle for this and are
authored-cream-shaped. Decision: **cream light bands, navy (`tailDark 0x2a3142`) rings, as
authored. The K4 expectation is updated to cream+navy; teal bands in any frame remain a
grade finding, SHADING's Band A.**

## 2. The 4.5:1 band ladder stays. ΔL≈1 in shadow is accepted — conditionally, and the
condition is named.

Rings surviving as hue-only inside the shadow band is what a 3-band cel ramp does: value is
quantised per band by construction, so in-shadow value contrast is a property of the *grade's*
shadow term, not of the albedo ratio. The albedo lever has a hard ceiling here: buying ΔL
through a clamped shadow light (`shadowTintPeak` — KNOWN_ISSUES §3: the only live daylight
magnitude lever) means pushing cream toward the near-white reserved for the glint/sclera
discipline and navy toward the near-black reserved for ink — both ends of a widened ladder
pay for shadow contrast by breaking other settled reads. Declined.

§2.3's "you can read detail inside shadows" therefore lands on SHADING's shadow
floor/transparency, where the headroom actually is, and SHADING's pre-registered
single-knob legs are already queued post-freeze.

**Condition:** this acceptance assumes the grade fix lands. On the first post-grade-fix
capture (the neutral-arm run), measure shaded-ring legibility at sly-closeup scale. If the
rings still do not read in shade under a *fixed* grade, the ladder question reopens — as its
own mini-seal with bands frozen at that calibration, not as a reflexive widening now. Until
that frame exists, no number is claimed for what "legible" is; freezing a threshold against
the current broken-grade frames would seal a band against pixels already known to be wrong.
