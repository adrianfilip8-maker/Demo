The landing rule: fast and not a move you meant

57c2c9e → 803a20asrc/player/Controller.js
Your verdict

“Landings seem fine, there should be no fall damage.” landHard 15.0 and the controlled-departure rule are ratified by play. No-fall-damage was already true of the build — the only damage sources are hazards and guard swings — and is now pinned by test R8: a 28.4 m/s crash costs zero health, with a hazard as the counterexample proving the test can see damage at all.

The short history: landings began as a coin flip (a race zeroed arrival speed before the probe read it), the first threshold derivation collapsed on the real level (the median walk-off arrives above any liveable threshold), every property of the fall failed to separate "meant" from "happened" — and the answer was a second term: a landing is hard only when it was fast and the descent began from a lost traversal beat, never from walking or jumping off. Of 188 driven walk-offs, 158 land soft at any speed; wall-cling losses from height still hit hard. Two late classification bugs (a jump pressed at a pole top filed as a lost grip; a stale flag riding through spire landings) are fixed and pinned.

The hook chain's difficulty: a 0.25 s window and two untaught inputs

the chain completes from the authored entrysix drivers failed it before the mechanism was found

The chain rests on three things the game never teaches: pump along your velocity (holding toward the target nets 2.03 m/s; along-velocity reaches 13.43), steer the flight (unsteered, 0 of 61 release phases grab), and hold jump through the flight (releasing early fires the jump-cut and takes a third of your speed). The bail window is ~0.25 s, recurring every ~2.3 s.

What to watch

Swing the chain cold. If you find the pump and the steer within a few attempts, it is a skill beat and the level stands. If you bounce off it, the levers in order: ring-only widening of hookAuto, ring spacing, a taught moment — never the swing constants, which sit under every hook in the game.

Attacks and ring release, fixed two ways

17d1a0csrc/core/Input.js · the pointer-lock click swallow

The game swallowed left clicks while a pointer-lock request was pending — and a request that "succeeds" never guarantees the browser grants it. On a denied or cooling-down machine that is every click, forever: attacks dead, and the click route off a ring dead with them. Now three separate failure signals stop the swallow, and F is attack — a route that never touches the mouse. A ring has four exits: Space, E, F, or crouch, each on a distinct code path.

The one observation to report either way

After clicking into the game, does moving the mouse turn the camera? That reads the pointer-lock state directly. Camera follows + attacks work: closed. Camera follows + attacks still dead: a different bug, and that fingerprint is what round two needs. Still trapped on a ring: which of Space/E/F/crouch you tried localizes the mechanism by itself.
